# 張園 / Zheungyuan Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 13 August 2026**

Zheungyuan / 張園 (“Zheungyuan”, “we”, “us” or “our”) provides village-house rental presentation, enquiry organisation and renter-to-property matching services in Hong Kong.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store and disclose personal data when you contact us, enquire about a property, communicate with us through WhatsApp, use our website, or otherwise interact with our rental services.

## Personal data we may collect
We may collect names and contact details, WhatsApp identifiers and messages, rental enquiry and viewing history, property information, rental requirements such as area/budget/floor/pets/parking/move-in timing, administrative labels/notes/dates, structured summaries derived from communications, and basic technical logs.

## How we use personal data
We use personal data to respond to enquiries, organise prospective renter and landlord/property records, understand rental requirements, match prospective renters with relevant properties, arrange viewings, prepare property listings, maintain business/security records and comply with applicable requirements.

We do not make final tenancy decisions using automated systems. AI-assisted analysis is used to organise information and assist matching; landlords and renters remain responsible for their own decisions and agreements.

## AI-assisted processing
We may use AI tools to summarise and structure rental-related communications. When we use our private Zheungyuan Custom GPT, relevant stored enquiry information may be transmitted to OpenAI for processing through ChatGPT. Structured results may then be saved back to our rental database.

## Service providers and disclosure
We may use Railway or similar hosting/database providers, OpenAI, WhatsApp / Meta and other technical providers reasonably necessary to operate the service. We do not sell personal data.

We may disclose information to a landlord, property owner, renter or other party involved in a potential rental transaction only to the extent appropriate for that transaction, or where reasonably necessary or legally required.

## Cross-border processing
Some providers may process or store data outside Hong Kong. We aim to limit information shared to what is reasonably necessary for the relevant service.

## Data retention
We retain personal data only for as long as reasonably necessary for rental matching, follow-up, record-keeping, dispute handling and legal or regulatory requirements. Historical rental-enquiry information may be retained where it remains reasonably useful for understanding rental demand or prior dealings. Information that is no longer reasonably required may be deleted or anonymised.

## Data security
We take reasonable technical and organisational steps to protect personal data against unauthorised or accidental access, processing, loss, alteration, disclosure or erasure. Internal rental data and GPT Actions are access-controlled.

## Access and correction
You may contact us to ask whether we hold personal data about you, request access, request correction of inaccurate data, ask us to update your rental status/requirements, or request deletion of information that is no longer reasonably required, subject to any lawful or operational reason for retention.

## Direct marketing
We will not use personal data for unrelated direct marketing in a manner that does not comply with applicable Hong Kong privacy requirements.

## Contact
For privacy, access or correction requests, contact Zheungyuan / 張園 using the contact details published on our website.
