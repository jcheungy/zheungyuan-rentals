Why v1 failed:
Chromium's SingletonLock is commonly a symlink. On a Railway redeploy that
symlink points to the old container hostname, so it becomes a broken symlink.
Node's fs.existsSync() returns false for a broken symlink, which meant v1
didn't remove it.

v2:
- searches the WhatsApp LocalAuth path
- removes SingletonLock, SingletonSocket and SingletonCookie directly
- works even when they are broken symlinks
- does NOT delete the WhatsApp authentication/session data
- keeps /labels and label-filtered /sync/chats

After deployment, the log should show:
Removed stale Chromium lock: ...
WhatsApp authenticated
WhatsApp ready

Do not delete the Railway volume.
